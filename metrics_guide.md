# Metrics Guide

All examples below use fictional data.

## Example calculations

-   **Contact response rate** = candidates who responded ÷ candidates
    contacted × 100
-   **Qualification rate** = candidates qualified ÷ candidates screened
    × 100
-   **Stage conversion** = records reaching the next stage ÷ records
    entering the previous stage × 100
-   **Data completeness** = records with all required fields ÷ total
    records × 100

## Example

If 40 candidates are contacted and 12 respond:

Response rate = 12 ÷ 40 × 100 = **30%**

## Good measurement practices

-   Define each stage before measuring it.
-   Use the same time window when comparing periods.
-   Show counts alongside percentages, especially for small samples.
-   Separate facts from hypotheses about why a metric changed.
-   Avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.
-   Never upload personal candidate data, contact details, or
    confidential company information to a public repository.
